Each row of my_ratings.csv is one student record from the UCI Student Performance dataset (Portuguese secondary students). Some rows are real students and some were generated by AI models.
For each row, write real or synthetic in the guess column. Judge each row on its own.

Columns:
- school: GP = Gabriel Pereira, MS = Mousinho da Silveira
- sex: F / M
- address: U = urban, R = rural
- famsize: LE3 = 3 or fewer people, GT3 = more than 3
- schoolsup: gets extra educational support from the school (yes / no)
- internet: has internet at home (yes / no)
- higher: wants to go on to higher education (yes / no)
- romantic: in a romantic relationship (yes / no)
- age: 15-22
- Medu: mother's education, 0 = none, 1 = primary (up to 4th grade), 2 = 5th-9th grade, 3 = secondary, 4 = higher
- studytime: weekly study time, 1 = under 2 h, 2 = 2-5 h, 3 = 5-10 h, 4 = over 10 h
- failures: classes failed before, 0-3 (3 = 3 or more)
- goout: going out with friends, 1 = very low ... 5 = very high
- health: current health, 1 = very bad ... 5 = very good
- absences: number of school absences, 0-93
- G1: first-term grade, 0-20
- passed: passed the year, i.e. final grade 10 or more out of 20 (yes / no)
- guess: my answer, real or synthetic
